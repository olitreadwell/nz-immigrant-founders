# Immigrant-founded New Zealand companies

A verified dataset of New Zealand companies founded by people who were born overseas and moved here.

Every entry records where the founder was born, when they arrived, what they founded, and links to evidence and news coverage.

**14 entries, 19 news links. Last verified 2026-10-22.**

## The rule

An entry must satisfy three tests.

1. The founder was born outside New Zealand.
2. The founder moved to New Zealand.
3. The founder started or co-founded a business here.

Being the New Zealand-born child of immigrants does not count. That rules out some famous names, and the [exclusions](#exclusions) section lists them.

## Dataset

- `data/founders.json` is the source of truth.
- `data/founders.csv` is a flat version for spreadsheets.
- `schema.json` describes the JSON shape.
- `scripts/validate.py` checks required fields, enums, unique IDs and URL shape.

Run the checks with:

```
python3 scripts/validate.py
```

## Verification standard

Each entry carries at least one evidence item with a claim, a named source, and a URL.

`confidence` is `high` when the birthplace and the founding are both stated in a primary or company source. It is `medium` when a single secondary source is all that was found.

Where a source does not establish a fact, the field is `null` and the note says so. Guesses are not recorded as data.

## What this is not

This is not a count of businesses owned by ethnic communities. That is a different measure, and it includes many New Zealand-born people.

For the statistical picture, see the Waitakere Ethnic Board report with MBIE and the Ministry for Ethnic Communities: ethnic minority communities contributed $87 billion to GDP in 2023, 22 per cent of the total.

The entrepreneurship rate for most migrant and ethnic groups sits below the Pākehā rate. Migration explains the population share of these businesses better than an unusual appetite for risk does.

## Exclusions

These people are often described as immigrant founders. They were born in New Zealand, so they are excluded.

- **Woolf Fisher**, co-founder of Fisher & Paykel. Born in Wellington. His father Michael was a Jewish immigrant from Shumsk, now in Ukraine.
- **Sir George Fistonich**, founder of Villa Maria. Born in New Zealand in 1939 to parents from Hvar, Croatia.
- **Sam Morgan** (Trade Me), **Rod Drury** (Xero) and **Peter Beck** (Rocket Lab). All New Zealand-born.

## Known gaps

- The Entrepreneur Work Visa was closed to new applicants in August 2025. It had low application numbers and high decline rates, so it is not a good source of founder counts.
- No official dataset records the birthplace of company founders. Stats NZ business demography and the Companies Registry do not capture it.
- Several well-known candidates remain unverified: Todd Corporation, Hannahs, Kirkcaldie & Stains, Speight's, Fulton Hogan, 2degrees and Volpara. They are not in the dataset because the birthplace could not be confirmed.
- Paywalled articles are cited by headline and URL. Some bodies were not readable during verification.
- Some publishers block automated requests. A 403 or a dropped connection from a script does not mean the link is dead. The URLs below were opened and read during verification, sometimes through a text proxy.
- `scripts/validate.py` checks structure, not reachability. Link checking lives outside CI because too many NZ news sites reject bots.

## Contributing

Add one entry to `data/founders.json`, then run `python3 scripts/validate.py`.

Every new entry needs a birthplace source, an arrival source, a founding source, and at least one news link where one exists.
